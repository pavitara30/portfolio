IoT Hovercraft with Voice Control

A hovercraft you can drive by talking to it, built during the ICAM Paris student exchange.

The idea
Make a hovercraft that steers itself around obstacles and takes orders by voice or phone.
What I did
Programmed the Arduino in Embedded C, wired ultrasonic sensors for obstacle detection, and built a Blynk app for remote control and monitoring.
How it went
A working prototype with voice navigation, shipped in one month abroad.

Embedded C · Arduino · Ultrasonic sensors · Blynk · Android
